Our Story

Driven by Precision, Powered by People

Founded in 2010, Swift Logistics Solutions started with a simple mission: make logistics faster, smarter, and more reliable for businesses of all sizes. What began as a small freight forwarding operation has grown into a global logistics partner with presence in 30+ countries.

Today, we serve over 500 businesses worldwide, handling everything from international freight to complex supply chain management. Our team of 200+ logistics professionals works tirelessly to ensure every shipment arrives on time, every time.

We believe in transparency, innovation, and above all else, putting our clients first.

Our Journey

Company Timeline

2010

Founded in Addis Ababa

Swift Logistics Solutions was established with a single truck and a vision to transform logistics in Ethiopia.

2013

Regional Expansion

2017

Global Reach

Launched international freight services, partnering with global carriers to reach 15+ countries.

2022

Digital Transformation

Implemented AI-powered tracking, automated warehousing, and a client portal for real-time visibility.

30+ Countries
